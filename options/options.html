Screenshots

Where captures go and how files are named.

Relative to Chrome’s download location.

Filename tokens
  • {datetime} — date and time
  • {title} — page title
  • {url} — full hostname (e.g. www.example.com)
  • {domain} — base name without TLD (e.g. example)
  • {subdomain} — labels except TLD (e.g. www_example)
  • {type} — VP / DL / FS / RG
